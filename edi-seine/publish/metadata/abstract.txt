This dataset contains fish catch and habitat observations from beach seine surveys of the lower Feather River,
California, collected between 1997 and 2025. Surveys were conducted at 42 river-mile sites
(225 distinct recorded location names) between river mile 12.5 and 66.5, in both the low flow and high
flow channels, to monitor the fish community and the habitat where juvenile fish, particularly juvenile
Chinook salmon, were found. [TODO: state who conducted the surveys and the purpose or monitoring program.]
Data were compiled from three source periods (1997-2001, 2008-2014 and 2015-2025) and standardized into a
single table of 60,699 records from about 2,000 seine samples. Sampling occurred in 1997-2001, 2008-2009,
2013-2014 and 2015-2025; no data are included for 2002-2007 or 2010-2012. Most sampling occurred between
January and August. Each record is a fish species (and, from 2008 onward, a count by length) captured in a
seine haul, with fork length, life stage, Chinook salmon run and adipose fin clip status where recorded.
Each record also carries the sample's water temperature, haul dimensions, depth, substrate, cover, stream
feature, gear size and river mile. In total 42 species or taxonomic groups were recorded; Chinook salmon,
Sacramento sucker and Sacramento pikeminnow were the most frequently recorded. Observations from 1997-2001
are one row per individual fish, whereas 2008-2025 rows carry a count. Measurement units for haul dimensions
and depth differ between source periods and are described in the attribute definitions. These data are
published to support the Healthy Rivers and Landscapes Science Program.
